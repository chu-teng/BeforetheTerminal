# Before the Terminal Guide

**English** | [简体中文](GUIDE.zh-CN.md)

## Rules

### Introduction

Press K, or click the button at the top left of your inventory during a round, to read this guide. Survive aboard an express train bound for the terminal station and uncover the truth before it arrives.

Passengers must eliminate all murderers and outsiders, or survive until arrival. Murderers must eliminate all passengers and outsiders. Outsiders must eliminate all passengers and murderers, or survive until arrival. Loner factions have independent objectives; their survival or death does not determine other factions' victory.

You know your role, traits and the current encounter. You wake in your room with a Room Key and Letter. Looking at another player reveals their name; voice chat reaches nearby players. Use your Letter to open the grimoire, which lists portraits, seat numbers, names and category counts. Annotate each player's faction, role, death or disappearance. While holding your Letter, players in view glow according to your annotations. Encounters are special rules randomly chosen for a round.

### Roles

**Categories.** There are seven: Townsfolk, Villagers, Demons, Minions, Travelers, Loners and Outsiders. Townsfolk have some means of establishing their identity. Most Travelers belong to passengers and a few to murderers, but their abilities weaken passengers. Role prefixes are T, V, D, M, R, L and O; the final A, B or C identifies the script.

**Seats.** For n players, // means integer division rounded down. Murderers = n // 6; Travelers = 1 + n // 8; Loners = n // 8; there is one Outsider at 12 or more players. All remaining seats are passengers. There is exactly one Demon. Split passenger seats equally between Townsfolk and Villagers, giving the extra seat to Townsfolk. A standard 18-player round has 5 Townsfolk, 4 Villagers, 1 Demon, 2 Minions, 3 Travelers, 2 Loners and 1 Outsider. Roles normally do not repeat unless an encounter permits it. In Memory and Custom modes, use unplayed eligible roles first; once the available role pool is exhausted, roles may repeat to fill the remaining seats.

**Factions.** Only passengers drop guns and Master Keys on death, and only passengers can pick them up. Murderer instinct reveals everyone: murderers red, others green. Outsider instinct reveals fellow faction members in their role colors. Roles and factions can change. An Townsperson who joins another faction after losing their gun or bow receives it again; returning to passengers removes it. Other roles do not join solo factions. A Loner joining another faction cannot win independently through their solo objective, but can still use abilities after completing it. A perceived role may differ from the real role: this is a perception override. Some roles borrow abilities or temporarily change role. Your initially assigned identity is your initial role.

### Round Flow

**Executions.** A passenger killing with a gun (including a Derringer), bow or knife performs an execution. Executing a passenger is a wrongful kill; only a wrongful kill with a bow normally causes suicide. An execution costs 40 sanity and makes the executor drop their gun. Shields, invincibility and execution exemptions prevent the applicable death and play a shield-breaking sound. Exemption only blocks executions; wrongful-kill checks still apply.

**Death.** Death leaves a body, which some roles can remove. Causes include accident, mental breakdown, gunshot, arrow, suicide, stabbing, grenade, blunt weapon, poison, magic, mishap, breach, axe, bomb, flying sword, dagger, explosion, curtain call, bloom and martyrdom.

**Epilogues.** When murderers and outsiders together outnumber passengers, the countdown drops to two minutes. An epilogue begins below two minutes, accompanied by music. An Outsider hosts their epilogue while present; otherwise, or if the host dies with no replacement, the survival epilogue belongs to passengers. The featured faction wins when its epilogue ends.

**Endings.** Passenger victory has separate announcements for eliminating all murderers and outsiders before arrival, or reaching the terminal first. Murderer victory has separate announcements depending on whether the Demon survived or Minions completed the task after the Demon died. Black Death and Heretic have special faction endings. Simultaneous objectives prioritize Conductor, Black Death, Loner, Outsider, murderer, then passenger victory. Relationship traits do not create separate ending titles.

### Abilities

**Sanity.** The passenger resource starts at 100 and drains naturally. Medium sanity causes incorrect item appearances and drunkenness; low sanity also causes incorrect skins. At zero, you die by mental breakdown. At high sanity, force a skill still on cooldown for 40 + remaining cooldown seconds // 4 sanity. This bypasses cooldown without restoring spent once-only uses.

**Needs.** Completing a need restores 20 sanity. Needs are eating, drinking, sleeping, fresh air, company, solitude, bathing and music, with up to four accumulated. After your sixth completed need, a recovery need asks you to return to your room, operate the control panel or inspect the lookout seat. It awards a random Door Bolt, Crowbar, Remote or Walkie-Talkie, excluding any of these present in your starting inventory.

**Madness.** Murderers, Outsiders and Loners use madness in their ability-specific shops. Gain 5 every ten seconds, stopping natural income above 200, and 100 for a kill. Wrongful kills grant Demons 100 and Minions 50. Passengers and Loners have limited stamina; murderers and Outsiders have unlimited stamina. Only murderers and Outsiders normally see the countdown. It begins at eight minutes and gains one minute per passenger death.

**Abilities.** Angle brackets mark active skills. Self skills use the skill key; nearby skills use it on a nearby player; remote skills select players in the inventory menu. Unless specified otherwise, skills begin on cooldown; skills without a normal cooldown start with 30 seconds. Talents are passive. Shops are part of role abilities. Role entries list equipment or unconditional properties, then talents, then skills.

**Drunkenness.** You are not told when you become drunk. Skills, talents and shops fail: non-information effects disappear and direct or indirect information becomes incorrect. Drunk murderers cannot spend madness. Equipment, instinct, and obvious abilities usable unconditionally at any time remain functional, including some actions bound to the skill key.

**Conversion.** When a Townsperson joins another faction after having dropped their gun or bow, they receive it again; returning to the passenger faction removes it. Other roles cannot join a Loner faction. A Loner joining another faction can no longer win through their Loner objective, but may continue using their abilities after meeting it. Some roles perceive a role different from their technical role; this is a perceived-role override. Some use another role's abilities, and some temporarily become another role. The role first assigned to them is their initial role.

### Setting

Rooms contain a bed and a plate with a Note; first-class rooms also contain Firecrackers.

The locomotive control panel shows the countdown and living-player count. Speech while using it is broadcast throughout the train.

The lookout seat grants green instinct revealing everyone, but drains sanity at eight times normal.

The chandelier trap activates for five seconds and can crush players beneath it. The wire trap activates for five seconds and electrocutes players in its area.

### Modes

**Script Mode:** 6–14 players, designed around 12. Randomly select and announce one script. Townsperson guns and bows become single-use, as do Veteran and Tlamacazqui knives; Witch has no knife. Murderers cannot buy weapons. Reporting a body starts a 30-second meeting, highlighting bodies from the current meeting interval, followed by 30 seconds of voting. The highest-voted player is executed with at least half the living votes. A qualifying tie instead makes tied players drunk until the next meeting.

A — Locked-Room Murders

B — Sects & Sinners

C — The Dreamer's Diary

**Draft Mode:** 6–22 players, designed around 18. In random order, choose one of three visible role slips or a hidden random slip. Visible choices announce the role's presence; random choices remain hidden. Only passenger and murderer factions remain: Loners join passengers, Outsiders join murderers, and the Demon's death gives passengers victory. Selected roles do not repeat in Draft Mode. Custom rounds using Draft allow repeats after the eligible pool is exhausted. A timeout has a one-in-four chance of selecting the hidden slip. Marionette and Lunatic draft perceived roles; Heretic and Black Death are always hidden when announced.

**Memory Mode:** 6 or more players. Opening and skill feedback conceal role identity; skill results give only result parameters or Yes/No. Guess your role with a Slip every 30 seconds. Unrelated means both script and category are wrong; Related means one is correct; Close means both are correct; Perfect means the role is correct. A perfect guess makes you glow to others for five seconds. Guns and bows follow the same execution rules: passengers keep guns and wrongful kills cause suicide.

**Mob Mode:** 6 or more players, without encounters. Split players between pink and pale-blue factions. Each faction fills seats in this order: Demon, Minion, Townsperson, Villager, Outsider, Minion, Townsperson, Loner, then continues the category pattern. An unmatched extra player is always a Villager. Eliminate the opposing faction, or have more survivors when time expires; equal survivor counts produce a draw. Everyone has unlimited stamina, no sanity drain, access to the countdown and instinct revealing only their own faction.

**Custom Mode:** 6 or more players. Choose one base rule: Script, Draft or Memory. Independently enable Debate, Duel and Judgment. Script Mode combines Script + Debate; Draft Mode combines Draft + Duel; Memory Mode combines Memory + Judgment. Mob rules cannot be selected here.

### Encounters

**Dream:** With three players left, they use Slips to decide whether to restart. If approved, restart immediately with the same roles and a two-minute countdown.

**Storm:** Announce a possible passenger role as the eye of the storm. That role can only die by execution. If present, murderers also learn who it is.

**Rebirth:** A Loner completing their objective does not end the game, but joins the final winners. Interaction: Monk.

**Complicity:** If murderers remain when an Outsider epilogue ends, murderers win and the Outsider faction joins the winners.

**Six Paths:** The first two passengers to die revive as unplayed Loner roles.

**Duplicates:** Two players may share a role.

**Perfect Goodness:** Everyone receives a Slip listing the round's roles. Interactions: Heretic and Black Death.

**Painted Skin:** The Demon learns three unplayed passenger roles at the start.

## Items

### Free Items

**[Food and Drinks]** Fulfill needs.

**[Note]** Write on it and place it on a wall, floor or ceiling.

**[Firecracker]** Produces gunshot sounds and is invisible to everyone except the player who placed it.

**[Fake Gun]** Looks like a real gun and can produce gunshot sounds.

**[Room Key]** Opens the door to a room.

**[Letter]** Right-click to open the Grimoire, which lists every player's portrait, number and name. You can annotate their faction, role and status, such as dead or missing. It also shows the number of players in each role category for the round. While holding the letter, players in view glow according to your annotations.

### Passenger Items

**[Gun]** Cooldown: 10 seconds after a miss, one minute after a hit.

**[Bow]** Must be drawn. No cooldown after a miss; one minute after a hit. A wrongful kill causes suicide.

**[Master Key]** Opens any door. Held by one random Townsperson and certain neutrals.

**[Door Bolt]** Jams a door for one minute. Cooldown: two minutes.

**[Remote]** Activates the nearest mechanism within 20 blocks. Cooldown: one minute.

### Non-Passenger Items

**[Knife]** Requires charging. Murderer cooldown: one minute.

**[Walkie-Talkie]** Initially held by all murderers and Undercover. You must hold it to transmit, but not to listen.

**[Crowbar]** Breaks doors. Available in shops.

The Minion shop sells: Poison Gas Grenade 200, Psychosis Mask 350, Derringer 400, Poison 25, Scorpion 25, Remote 100, Door Bolt 25, Master Key 50, Body Bag 50 and Circuit Breaker 400. Delinquent, Marionette, Traitor and Black Death do not receive this shop merely for belonging to the murderer faction.

**[Poison]** Poisons a plate. A poisoned player hears a heartbeat warning and dies after one minute.

**[Scorpion]** Poisons a bed. Adjacent beds forming a double bed need only one application. A poisoned bed poisons anyone who sleeps in it, every time.

**[Psychosis Mask]** Enters psychosis mode for 30 seconds: your appearance changes, you gain one shield, and you use a bat with a charged attack to kill.

**[Derringer]** Holds one bullet. If you miss, killing someone by any means replenishes it.

**[Body Bag]** Removes a body.

**[Circuit Breaker]** Cuts power throughout the train, from anywhere.

Demon shop: Crossbow 250, Grenade 350, Gun 400, Crowbar 25, Fake Gun 25, Master Key 50, Micro Walkie-Talkie 50, Body Bag 50, Swiftness II Potion 150, Circuit Breaker 400.

Loner shop: Master Key 50, Wind Charge 150, Ender Pearl 150, Night Vision Potion 50, Swiftness II Potion 150, Invisibility Potion 300.

Outsider shop: Door Bolt 25, Crowbar 25, Poison 25, Scorpion 25, Remote 100, Fake Gun 25, Wind Charge 150, Ender Pearl 200.

**[Micro Walkie-Talkie]** Uses the normal radio texture. Other players cannot see it held; speech reaches only radio listeners, including nearby listeners. Swiftness Potions are level II. Drinking a potion in a BTT round automatically recycles the glass bottle.

**[Crossbow]** Piercing, multishot and single-use.

**[Poison Gas Grenade]** Releases poisonous gas that fills a room.

T2A【Railway Police】Starts with a [Gun]. Does not drop it after an execution. Unlimited sanity and stamina.

T3A【Witch】Starts with a [Knife] usable three times. &lt;Curse&gt; any player other than yourself so they die with you when you are killed, excluding suicide. Changing the target has a one-minute cooldown.

T4A【Veteran】Starts with a [Knife].

T5A【Engineer】Starts with a [Door Bolt]. Place an invisible <Camera> that continually reports passing players. Recover it to place it elsewhere.

T6A【Architect】&lt;Open Passage&gt; at your current location. After 30 seconds, you can teleport back there at any time. Cooldown: one minute.

T7A【Postman】Other players can right-click you with an item in hand to give it to you. Right-click another player while holding an item to give it to them.

T8A【Collector】Instinct reveals dropped items and placed notes. &lt;Summon&gt; the nearest dropped item, making it fly into your hand. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

T1B【Hunter】Starts with a [Gun]. Once per game, &lt;Snipe&gt; any player by identifying them as the Demon. If correct, they die.

T2B【Judge】Starts with a [Gun]. &lt;Prosecute&gt; by selecting the Demon first, then every Minion. If correct, your faction wins. Cooldown: one minute.

T3B【Chess Player】Starts with a [Gun]. Has the Minion shop and passive madness income. Each completed need grants 50 madness; each executed neutral grants 200.

T4B【Tlamacazqui】Starts with a [Knife] usable three times. After you commit a wrongful kill, everyone is invulnerable for 30 seconds. Self-inflicted deaths still apply.

T5B【Undercover】An ordinary passenger, except you start with a [Walkie-Talkie] and appear red to murderer instinct. Recovery will not grant a second Walkie-Talkie.

T6B【Puppeteer】Select two players. If the first uses a targeted ability, all its targets are redirected to the second.

T7B【Craftsman】&lt;Replicate&gt; an item held by another player. After 30 seconds, you can give it to someone else. Cooldown: one minute.

T8B【Smuggler】&lt;Smuggle&gt; a nearby player into a possession state like Kidnapper. There is no countdown or digestion, and release does not reduce sanity based on time spent inside. Their voice originates from your position, without a separate channel. You may release them after 30 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds. Death releases them.

T1C【Caballero】Starts with a [Bow].

T2C【Xia】Starts with a [Bow]. When using it, you die by suicide if the kill is not wrongful, and survive if it is wrongful.

T3C【Night Watchman】Starts with a [Gun]. Permanent night vision. Players entering psychosis glow for you. On death, enters a five-second dying state.

T4C【Aviateur】When killed, leaves a body, revives in their room, receives a [Gun] and becomes invisible to murderer instinct.

T5C【Mercenary】Belongs to the passengers, but otherwise has the role and abilities of a random unplayed Minion. Uses the Minion shop, gains passive madness and 100 per kill, but none from misexecutions.

T6C【Snake Charmer】&lt;Charm&gt; any player. If they are the Demon, swap roles and factions with them. The new Snake Charmer becomes permanently drunk. Cooldown: one minute.

T7C【Cannibal】&lt;Devour&gt; and remove a body. A passenger body turns you into its role normally for one minute. A non-passenger body turns you into a random passenger role not in play, drunk for one minute. Cooldown: one minute.

T8C【Philosopher】Once per game, &lt;Speculate&gt; any passenger role in the script and become that role. If it is present, its player is drunk until you die.

### Villagers

V1A【Doctor】Can see a body's role, cause of death and time of death.

V2A【Noble】You have three main-family members: two passengers and one non-passenger. With at least 12 players, you also have four branch-family members: a Villager, a Minion, a Loner and an Outsider. The groups never overlap; looking at someone identifies membership. While drunk, each group is randomly drawn from outside its original list, still without overlap.

V3A【Pharmacist】Recognizes poisoned plates and beds. Poisoned players in view glow for you. &lt;Medicate&gt; a player to cure their poison, or fully restore their sanity if healthy. Cooldown: one minute.

V4A【Bartender】Choose a role to &lt;Pour&gt;. If present, all players of that role become drunk for two minutes. Cooldown: two minutes.

V5A【Journalist】&lt;Track&gt; any player to see them through walls until you change targets. Cooldown: one minute.

V6A【Scribe】Record the names of abilities and triggered talents used in the round, keeping the latest four. &lt;Interpret&gt; the records, then pause recording for 30 seconds. Names only; users are not revealed.

V7A【Maid】Players in your view with at least three needs glow. Each non-passenger within 20 blocks adds one times the normal sanity drain; with four nearby, you die from mental breakdown.

V8A【Detective】&lt;Examine&gt; a nearby player to learn whether they have killed. Alternatively, select two players in order: &lt;Deduction&gt; succeeds if the first is dead and was killed by the second, and fails otherwise. Cooldown: one minute.

V1B【Mortician】Instinct reveals passenger bodies in green and non-passenger bodies in red. While you live, a Minion death makes everyone drunk for 30 seconds.

V2B【Folklorist】Players in view glow for you when they use a remote ability. Whenever someone is selected by a remote ability, you learn the target's name.

V3B【Medium】&lt;Retain&gt; a player. After death, they continue to possess you. You can choose another only after the previous target becomes a spirit. When you die, the possessing spirit returns to life through your body and becomes permanently drunk.

V4B【Monk】<Convert> a nearby player. A Loner completing their objective joins the final winners without ending the round. Cooldown: one minute. Under Duel, completing a converted Loner objective also grants one shield.

V5B【Virgin】Exempt from execution. <Honor> a nearby player so their wrongful kill always causes suicide. Cooldown: one minute. Under Judgment, honoring a non-passenger instead makes you permanently drunk.

V6B【Star】On death, glows for the passenger faction for 30 seconds, but not for other factions. &lt;Mark&gt; any player so that they glow with you when you die. Changing the target has a 30-second cooldown.

V7B【Professor】Use &lt;Vial&gt; to infuse a plate with a potion. Whoever consumes it gains one shield. Cooldown: two minutes.

V8B【Bodyguard】&lt;Escort&gt; a nearby player for 30 seconds: they can die only if you die. Cooldown: two minutes.

V1C【Investigator】&lt;Investigate&gt; a nearby player to learn one passenger role and one murderer role. The target is one of these; if they are a solo neutral or outsider, they are neither. Cooldown: one minute.

V2C【Prophet】<Prophesy> three players other than yourself to learn whether any is the Demon. Selecting the same player twice instead compares that player with the remaining player: Yes if both are passengers or both non-passengers, otherwise No. Cooldown: one minute.

V3C【Muyuubyou】Enter or leave a <Dream> at any time, observing a grayscale world through a free soul camera. Every time you get out of bed, automatically sleepwalk: after ten seconds, your body teleports to the camera position.

V4C【Dreamer】Every time you get out of bed, you automatically enter &lt;Lucid Dream&gt; with grayscale vision. Those who die possess you and share a separate voice channel that excludes you. Bodies remain and endings proceed normally. After two minutes, you wake without rewinding. If killed during the dream, excluding suicide, round state rewinds, dream victims revive and you remain dead.

V5C【Conductor】Can see the countdown. At the start, learn whether murderers cluster toward the front (lower seat numbers) or rear (higher numbers), comparing their average number with the full roster midpoint. Only real Demons and Minions count; ties are random. Surviving until the game ends wins for your faction.

V6C【Attendant】Can see the countdown. At the start, learn whether the nearest real Demon or Minion by seat-number difference is toward the front or rear; ties are random. While you live, the countdown runs twice as fast.

V7C【Devushka】Immune to instinct vision. Can &lt;Play Dead&gt;.

V8C【Poppy Grower】While you live, murderer instinct shows everyone green. Direct inspection remains normal.

## Murderers

### Demons

D1A【Assassin】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Identify&gt; another player's role. A correct guess kills them; an incorrect guess exposes your own role. Cooldown: one minute.

D2A【Duce】Starts with a [Knife]. Looking at a player reveals their role. Heretic appears as a random Villager.

D3A【Harpy】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Shroud&gt; two different living players in order. Everyone is told the first target. If the first survives one minute, the second dies. If the second is executed, the murderers win. Cooldown: two minutes.

D4A【Harlot】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Bewitch&gt; any player, making them drunk for 30 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

D1B【Magician】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Swap&gt; any two players' positions. Cooldown: one minute.

D2B【Rigger】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Bind&gt; any two players for 30 seconds. Cooldown: one minute.

D3B【Imp】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Imprint&gt; a nearby player. If they are a neutral, they become a murderer-faction Imp when you die. Changing the target has a one-minute cooldown.

D4B【Leech】Starts with a [Knife]. Once per game, &lt;Parasitize&gt; a nearby player. They are permanently drunk, and you can die only when they die.

D1C【Sculptor】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Rebuild&gt; any passenger or murderer into a passenger or murderer role not currently in play, without changing their faction. Cooldown: two minutes.

D2C【Barber】Starts with a [Knife]. Each neutral death grants one opportunity to <Exchange> two non-neutrals, exchanging roles. Opportunities do not accumulate.

D3C【Revolucionario】Starts with a [Knife]. While you live, executed players enter a five-second dying state and receive a Derringer they cannot drop.

D4C【Werewolf】Starts with a [Knife]. While you live, passengers treat all neutrals and murderers as Demon or Minions. Three misexecutions cause a murderer victory.

### Minions

M1A【Stowaway】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Hide&gt; for 30 seconds, or &lt;Forge Corpse&gt; to place the chosen player's body at your position with stabbing as its cause of death. Both abilities share a one-minute cooldown.

M2A【Actor】Starts with a [Knife] and can <Feign Death>. Select a living player and a living or dead appearance source to <Disguise> the first as the second. Changing selection has a one-minute cooldown.

M3A【Toymaker】Starts with a [Bear Trap]. Right-click to place it at your feet; it arms after you leave. A sneaking player dismantles it; otherwise it traps the player for ten seconds, draining sanity four times as fast. Trap cooldown: 30 seconds. &lt;Envenom&gt; your next trap so its victim is poisoned. Ability cooldown: one minute.

M4A【Alchemist】Starts with a [Poison Needle], poisoning a nearby player, plate or bed on a 30-second cooldown. Instinct reveals poisoned players in Alchemist's role color. Place a <Gas Package> at your feet; after you leave, it arms and releases poison gas when someone enters its two-meter radius, consuming the package. Skill cooldown: two minutes.

M5A【Terrorist】Starts with a [Bomb], on a 30-second cooldown. After activation it becomes visible in five seconds, can be passed after holding it for three seconds, and explodes after 15 seconds. Place a <Mine> at your feet; it arms after you leave and explodes when someone enters its two-meter radius. The blast resembles a grenade with a two-meter damage radius. Skill cooldown: two minutes.

M1B【Mastermind】Starts with a [Knife]. While you live, a Demon death reduces the timer to two minutes. The faction of the first player executed afterwards loses.

M2B【Cleaner】Starts with a [Dagger]. Silent, no charge time, one-minute cooldown. &lt;Cleanse&gt; dropped items. Cooldown: two minutes.

M3B【Colonel】Starts with a [Gun]. Toggle &lt;Feign Virtue&gt; at any time: executions then follow passenger rules, including dropping and picking up guns, but never count as wrongful kills.

M4B【Lawyer】Starts with a [Knife]. &lt;Defend&gt; a nearby player against one execution. Cooldown: one minute. Each player can be defended only once.

M5B【Warden】Starts with a [Cage]. Select three players with it; after a short delay, they enter the cage and become deaf and mute. They have 15 seconds to choose life or death. If all choose life, all die.

M1C【Librarian】Starts with a [Knife]. <Isolate> a nearby player after a delay, making them deaf and mute for 30 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds. While isolated, the louder someone speaks to them, the faster the speaker loses sanity, up to eight times normal.

M2C【Comedian】Starts with a [Balloon]. Use it to raise a nearby player's voice pitch after a delay. Cooldown: 30 seconds. A second pitch change makes them explode. &lt;Inflate&gt; a nearby player so that attempting an execution makes them explode. Cooldown: one minute.

M3C【Kheshig】Starts with a [Sword]. Throw a flying sword that pierces a line of players. Cooldown: two minutes.

M4C【Psychopath】Starts with a [Fire Axe]. Drawing it opens five seconds of uncharged killing, followed by a two-minute item cooldown.

M5C【Psychic】Starts with an [Amulet]. Hold right-click for two seconds to charge a kill through walls. Your view cannot turn while charging; the server keeps the initial aim. Item cooldown: two minutes. Instinct reveals your marked target in Psychic's role color. The first other player to use an active role skill becomes your target. They die on reaching medium sanity. Outside your sight, natural sanity drain pauses, while active costs and recovery remain possible. After their death, the next skill user becomes your target.

## Neutrals

### Travelers

R1A【Saint】If a passenger or a meeting vote executes you, your current faction loses.

R2A【Hermit】Starts with one shield. Surviving to the end makes you a winner; otherwise, you lose.

R3A【Delinquent】Belongs to the murderer faction. Starts with a [Knife]. Has no Walkie-Talkie and appears green to murderer instinct; otherwise behaves like other murderers.

R4A【Gambler】&lt;Wager&gt; absent roles equal to half the player count, then become the final correct role, including equipment. A wrong guess gives a 30-second cooldown. In Script Mode, instead guess half the roles actually absent.

R1B【Damsel】All Minions receive a single-use Derringer. A miss or a hit on anyone else causes no kill and consumes it. If it shoots you dead, your current faction loses.

R2B【Politician】Someone about to target you with a skill becomes drunk for one minute, then you join their faction. Cooldown: one minute. This interception does not trigger while on cooldown.

R3B【Marionette】Belongs to the murderer faction. You believe you are a passenger role not otherwise in play, but you are actually drunk.

R4B【Heretic】Belongs to the passenger faction. Swaps the passenger and murderer victory results, even after your death.

R1C【Traitor】Belongs to the murderer faction. Otherwise has the abilities of a random Villager not in play.

R2C【Lunatic】You believe you are a Demon not otherwise in play. True Demons glow green to you; murderers see you in your role color. You are not drunk, but spending madness also deducts it from Demons, even below zero.

R3C【Amnesiac】Once per game, &lt;Inherit&gt; and remove a body to become its role, including items, and faction.

R4C【Black Death】Starts in the murderer faction. When killed, possess the killer, who becomes your patient, and use the murderer radio channel. A killed patient passes infection to their killer. Surviving as the infection until the three-player end condition gives murderers victory. Only the patient's suicide ends you, causing the other murderers to die by martyrdom. Your faction can change, but the special player-count, victory and martyrdom rules still affect murderers.

### Loners

L1A【Arsonist】Starts with a [Master Key]. Instinct reveals all doused players. &lt;Douse&gt; a nearby player with gasoline. Cooldown: 10 seconds; the notification is delayed. Win when all living passengers have been doused.

L2A【Thief】Starts with a [Master Key]. Instinct reveals bodies. &lt;Scavenge&gt; a body, removing it and briefly revealing all players through walls for 10 seconds. Cooldown: 10 seconds. Win after scavenging bodies equal to one third of the player count.

L3A【Astral】<Projection> leaves your body still while a soul camera observes the world. Waking starts a 30-second cooldown. Continually learn two living passenger roles and one living Minion role. Wake while your soul is near a matching player to kill them with magic. Three such kills win.

L1B【Jester】Execution triggers psychosis with shields equal to the number of previous wrongful kills. Everyone can track the executor. Kill the executor before psychosis ends to win; otherwise, die by curtain call. Under Debate, target the first player who voted for you instead.

L2B【Executioner】&lt;Vendetta&gt; a nearby player. After a short delay, they learn that someone has marked them with hatred. Have them executed to learn their role. Win after two marked targets have been executed.

L3B【Kidnapper】&lt;Kidnap&gt; a nearby player. Cooldown: one minute. They possess you until their sanity reaches zero. Your death releases all living captives. Digest two players to win.

L1C【Novelist】&lt;Deduce&gt; the role of any living or dead player. A correct guess is announced to everyone; an incorrect guess kills you. Win by correctly guessing the roles of one third of the players.

L2C【Angel】Once per game, &lt;Guard&gt; a nearby player, then gain two shields. All damage they would take is redirected to you. Win by dying because of this protection.

L3C【Messenger】While you live, non-passengers also lose sanity. Delayed &lt;Freeze&gt; makes a nearby player a cold source for one minute, glowing to everyone. Anyone within 20 blocks loses sanity four times as fast. If the source dies during this time, their body remains a permanent cold source. Two mental breakdowns while you live make you win.

### Outsiders

O1A【Messiah】Belongs to the Cult faction. &lt;Preach&gt; to any player by guessing their role. Cooldown: one minute. A correct guess turns them into a follower and recruits them to the Cult; an incorrect guess exposes your role. Cult members follow neutral rules for picking up guns and performing executions, and can see each other through instinct. When Messiah dies, followers die by martyrdom.

O2A【Gardener】Belongs to the Gardener faction. &lt;Plant&gt; flowers on the train. Cooldown: 30 seconds; flowers must be at least 20 blocks apart. Flowers have three stages: seed for 30 seconds, invisible to others; seedling for one minute, during which they can be uprooted; and mature flower, able to move and attack players, but killed by one hit.

O1B【Majo】Starts with a [Gun] and one shield. Whoever breaks the shield joins your coven.

O2B【Voodoo】While you live, dead Townsfolk and murderers become Remnants in your faction. A Remnant jumping off the train becomes a normal spectator. Remnants share a separate voice channel and cannot hear other living players. Your instinct reveals Remnants. <Resurrect> a Remnant temporarily with their former role; after 30 seconds they dissipate permanently as a member of your faction.

O1C【Godfather】All Loners join the mafia. You borrow an unplayed Loner's abilities. One Loner or the Godfather completing their objective wins for the mafia. Under Duel, the Godfather and Loners join the murderer faction instead.

O2C【Legion】Starts with a [Poison Needle]. Your poison recruits victims instead of killing them. Trying to poison a neutral kills you instead. When the Legion member carrying the needle dies, a random Legion member becomes Legion and receives the needle.

## Traits

### Relationship Traits

Trait pools use your faction at the start of the round and do not change when your faction changes. Marionette and Lunatic use their perceived faction. In Script Mode, Loners receive passenger traits and Outsiders receive murderer traits.

**Lovers:** Randomly assigned. You know your partner's name. When one dies, the other dies for love. If both survive until the game ends, both join the winners without changing the ending title.

**Archenemies:** Pairs one Villager with one Minion. Both know each other's name and role. If the passenger is executed, the murderer gains two shields.

**Twins:** Randomly assigned. You know your twin's name and role. Twins always share a faction. Both opening alignment and later conversion prioritize Outsider > Murderer > Passenger > Loner; the higher-priority faction is retained or adopted by both. If both are Outsiders, they share the same specific Outsider faction.

**Nemesis:** Pairs one passenger with one non-passenger. Both know each other's name. If the non-passenger dies first, the passenger becomes Majo. If the passenger dies first, the non-passenger becomes Jester.

### General Traits

**Tease:** On death, broadcasts a fake epilogue title and music belonging to an outsider not in play, for up to two minutes.

**Reserved:** Walking, eating, drinking and similar actions make no sound.

**Cleanly:** You can recognize poisoned plates and beds.

**Crush:** When you die, one passenger becomes drunk for two minutes.

**Afterglow:** When death is triggered, enter a five-second dying state.

**Lucky:** Whenever you take damage, you have a 50% chance to survive.

**Familiar:** Item cooldowns are halved.

**Excellent:** Ability cooldowns are halved.

**Sleight:** Your held item is hidden.

**Energetic:** You can jump.

**Childlike:** Your body becomes smaller.

**Light:** Permanent slow falling.

**Night Owl:** Permanent night vision.

**Impatient:** Movement speed is slightly increased.

**Spirit Sense:** You are notified whenever someone dies.

**Obstinate:** Your death is announced to everyone.

**Resonant:** If you survive until the epilogue, everyone glows during it.

**Allergic:** You are notified whenever someone uses a remote ability on you.

### Passenger Traits

**Desperation:** A damaging hit is blocked with a probability equal to your missing sanity fraction. Each successful block restores 40 sanity.

**Talkative:** Sanity does not decrease while you speak.

**Hoarding:** You can take two portions of food or drink.

**Gluttonous:** You finish food and drinks instantly.

**Hollow:** Reaching zero sanity does not kill you.

**Trust:** One passenger's grimoire initially marks you as a passenger.

**Anxious:** Needs are generated faster.

**Endurance:** Unlimited stamina.

**Optimist:** Unlimited sanity.

**Rhythm:** You can see the countdown.

### Murderer Traits

**Stealth:** Entering psychosis mode does not play music.

**Playful:** Killing a player who is speaking grants an extra 100 madness.

**Closure:** Players you kill with melee weapons leave no body.

**Imposture:** You can pick up guns and bows, but attempting to use one makes you explode.

**Premeditated:** Your grimoire begins with the roles of the other murderers marked.

**Slacker:** Fulfilling a fake need grants 25 madness.

**Greedy:** When a teammate dies, you inherit their madness.

**Anatomy:** You can examine bodies.

### Loner Traits

**Afterlife:** Start with one shield. When someone breaks it, you join their faction.

**Disguised:** Anyone about to use an ability on you becomes drunk for one second.

**Flawless:** You can die only by execution.

**Promotion:** If you survive until an epilogue, gain three shields and green instinct vision.

### Outsider Traits

**Last Wish:** Whoever kills you joins your faction.

**Bond:** One passenger starts as a member of your faction.

**Insight:** You have green instinct vision.

**Witness:** Your grimoire marks one Demon or Minion as belonging to the murderer faction.
